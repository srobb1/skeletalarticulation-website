What to tell us

Whatever you’re comfortable sharing. These help us answer you well:

  • Your pet’s name, what kind of animal they are, and roughly how big
  • What you’re hoping for, even if you aren’t sure yet
  • Where you are, so we can help with shipping
  • A photo or two, if you’d like
