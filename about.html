About us

A family, a farm, and a lot of animals

Skeletal Articulation is a family studio dedicated to careful, high-quality work. Our home, studio, and small farm are nestled in the mountains of northern Utah.

Our family includes humans and many other species: goats, alpaca, sheep, chickens, dogs, cats, fish, turtles, and frogs. We give them a lot of love and care, and we bring that same tenderness to every animal entrusted to us.

We believe in treating every animal with respect, and in letting as little as possible go to waste.
