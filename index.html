The ones we love most leave the deepest SkAr.

We’re a small family studio in northern Utah. We gently clean, preserve, and articulate the animals people love, so something of them can stay with you.

See our work How it works

Every animal who comes to us is cared for as one of our own: handled gently, with patience and respect, from the day they arrive until the day they come home to you.

Pet memorials

A lasting memorial for your companion, a full skeleton or a preserved skull, with keepsakes like paw prints and fur.

Skull cleaning

Gentle cleaning with Dermestid beetles, the careful method museums use, safe for even the most delicate bones.

Custom pieces

Dioramas, wall art, and one-of-a-kind pieces, including in the frames and mirrors you send us.

About us

A family, a farm, and a lot of animals

Our home is in the mountains of northern Utah, shared with goats, alpaca, sheep, chickens, dogs, cats, fish, turtles, and frogs.

We bring the same tenderness we give our own animals to every animal entrusted to us.
