Shipping guide

If your pet has just passed

We’re so sorry. There’s no rush. This page explains how to care for your pet now and how to send them to us safely, whenever you’re ready.

First, keep them cold

Freezing is the most important step. Gently curl your pet into a resting position, as small as is comfortable, and place them inside several plastic bags. Then freeze them thoroughly. Larger animals take longer to freeze all the way through.

If you don’t have a freezer large enough, many veterinarians can do this for you.

Once they’re frozen, you have time. You don’t need to decide anything else today, and you’re welcome to write to us with questions first.

Is it all right to ship a pet?

Yes. It’s perfectly legal and quite common to ship a pet for memorial services. There are a few simple best practices that keep them safe on the way, described below.

What you’ll need

  • A sturdy shipping box, heavy-duty plastic tote, or insulated cooler
  • Gel freezer packs (the blue reusable ice packs)
  • Insulation (pink wall insulation works best, but isn’t essential)
  • Something absorbent, like puppy pads
  • Plastic bags

Packing, step by step

  1. Line your box or tote with insulation. If you’re using a foam cooler, fit it snugly inside a sturdy box so it can’t crack.
  2. Lay the absorbent material inside. Puppy pads work best; paper towels or a blanket are fine too. Anything you send can be returned to you if you’d like.
  3. Make sure your pet is fully frozen. Wrap them in a blanket or towel and place them in plastic bags, each tied separately so nothing can leak.
  4. Place your pet in the box with the freezer packs as close to them as you can, so the cold stays where it’s needed.
  5. Fill any empty space with more insulation, pads, towels, or crumpled newspaper so nothing can shift.
  6. Seal the box and it’s ready to go.

When to ship

Ship on a Monday or Tuesday so the package doesn’t sit anywhere over a weekend. Overnight shipping isn’t necessary if everything is packed well. Two- or three-day shipping, depending on the weather, is enough and saves a lot of money.

Please let us know before you send anything, so we know to expect them.
